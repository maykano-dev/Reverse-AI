# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want ReverseAI to be a place where people can learn about reverse
engineering, security, and agents without being harassed or dismissed. We are
committed to a welcoming, respectful community regardless of experience,
identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and kind. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming malice.
- Share credit. Help newcomers understand why a design exists.
- Respect the law and the consent of the people whose software you analyze.
- Do not use the project to harass, stalk, or attack others.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing malware, stolen credentials, or exploit details publicly.
- Pressuring maintainers to weaken the sandbox or evidence rules.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
project space. It also applies when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report concerns to the maintainer through a private channel on GitHub. Reports
will be reviewed in good faith. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or ban
participants when necessary.
